Ajrumiyyah — Study Guide

The classical primer of Arabic naḥw by Ibn Ājurrūm (d. 723 AH), covered from Bāb al-Kalām through the four Tawābiʿ (Naʿt, ʿAṭf, Tawkīd, Badal). Each chapter gives the original matn, a translation, a structured breakdown, and worked examples. Works fully offline — tap a flashcard to flip.

١ Chapters (al-Abwāb)
Every bāb from al-Kalām → al-Iʿrāb → ʿAlāmāt → al-Muʿrabāt → al-Afʿāl → al-Marfūʿāt → at-Tawābiʿ. Matn + translation + breakdown + examples. Filterable by section.
٢ Reference Tables
The iʿrāb-signs matrix (rafʿ/naṣb/khafḍ/jazm × word type), the 10 nawāṣib and 18 jawāzim, the sisters of kāna / inna / ẓanna, the five nouns, and the maʿārif.
٣ Active Recall
Flashcards (definition recall + matn recall), a matching game (term ↔ definition), and a quiz with multiple-choice questions on definitions, signs, and classification.
★ How to study this
Step 1: Read each chapter's matn aloud (right-to-left) until it flows, then read the breakdown.
Step 2: Flashcards in Definition mode — produce the Ajrumiyyah definition from the term.
Step 3: Matching game to lock term ↔ definition pairs.
Step 4: Full Quiz; revisit any chapter you miss. Repeat the matn of weak chapters from memory.
